How to use this estimate

The estimate uses the vehicle, service category, mileage and region you enter. It is a planning guide, not an itemized quote or a vehicle diagnosis.

✓
Planning tip

Compare the estimate with a written workshop quote. For symptoms or safety-related work, ask a qualified technician to inspect the vehicle.

Model trained on supplied Chennai/Mumbai service records. The source dataset does not state its currency, so the estimate is shown in source units. Actual costs vary by vehicle condition, parts, labour and location.

✦
Data-backed, with limits

This model is trained on 1,139 service records for supported Honda and Toyota models, petrol or diesel, in Chennai or Mumbai. Other vehicles and categories are not covered.
